# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Thai taxpayers needing to calculate their annual income tax, deductions, and potential refunds or owed amounts. They need a simple, step-by-step tool that reduces the complexity of tax calculations.

## Product Purpose
TaxFlow simplifies the Thai personal income tax calculation process. It provides an intuitive wizard-based flow to input income and deductions, culminating in a clear summary of tax liability or refund. Success means the user feels confident and unconfused about their taxes.

## Brand Personality
Minimal, Modern, Clean, yet Trustworthy and Friendly. It should feel like a premium fintech product that is accessible to everyday people.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered government tax forms (e.g., standard PDF tax filings).
- Overly corporate banking apps with dense data tables.
- "AI-generated" generic templates (e.g., standard Tailwind SaaS cards with purple/blue gradients).

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over cleverness**: The numbers and tax logic must be immediately understandable.
- **Progressive disclosure**: Show only the fields needed for the current step (like the wizard flow).
- **Expert confidence**: The design should assure the user that the calculations are accurate and professional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for readability (e.g., ensuring text hits ≥4.5:1 against backgrounds).
- Clear, distinct focus states for forms.
